# Blockchain Study Tracker
 creator name- Sachin_dav1224


## Description

The Blockchain Study Tracker is a decentralized smart contract built on the Aptos blockchain that enables students to record and track their study progress in a transparent, immutable, and verifiable manner. This innovative solution leverages blockchain technology to create a permanent record of learning achievements, study sessions, and academic milestones.

The smart contract allows students to:
- Initialize their personal study tracking profile
- Log study sessions with hours studied
- Automatically calculate and maintain study streaks
- Store verifiable proof of their learning commitment on-chain

Key features include real-time tracking of total study hours, timestamped study sessions, current consecutive study streaks, and longest streak achievements. All data is stored securely on the blockchain, ensuring transparency and preventing manipulation of study records.

## Vision

Our vision is to revolutionize education accountability and motivation through blockchain technology. We aim to create a trustless ecosystem where:

- **Students** can build verifiable academic portfolios that showcase their dedication and consistency
- **Educational institutions** can access transparent student engagement metrics for admissions and scholarships
- **Employers** can verify candidates' self-learning capabilities and discipline
- **Parents and mentors** can monitor progress with complete transparency

We envision a future where consistent learning habits are rewarded through blockchain-verified achievements, creating a global standard for educational accountability that transcends traditional institutional boundaries.
